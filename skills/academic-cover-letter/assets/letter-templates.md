# 条件式英文投稿信模板

这些模板提供结构，不提供事实。所有方括号字段均需替换或有理由地删除。声明块只插入“本刊要求放在信中且作者已确认”的内容，参见 [声明规则](../references/declarations-and-checks.md)。通用模板不覆盖目标期刊的当前指南。

## A. 首次投稿

```text
[DATE]

Dear Editors,

We submit our manuscript, “[EXACT TITLE],” for consideration as a [ARTICLE TYPE] in [EXACT JOURNAL NAME]. [ONE SENTENCE DEFINING THE UNRESOLVED SCIENTIFIC OR PRACTICAL QUESTION].

In this [DESIGN] involving [RELEVANT POPULATION/SETTING], we [WHAT WAS ACTUALLY DONE]. [PRIMARY FINDING, WITH AN ESTIMATE AND UNCERTAINTY WHEN APPROPRIATE]. [EXPLAIN THE SPECIFIC ADDITION TO EXISTING EVIDENCE WITHOUT CLAIMING UNSUPPORTED PRIORITY].

The findings are relevant to [THE JOURNAL'S SPECIFIC READERS] because [LINK THE RESULT TO A REAL KNOWLEDGE GAP OR DECISION]. [STATE AN IMPORTANT BOUNDARY IF IT CHANGES THE INTERPRETATION].

[INSERT ONLY VERIFIED DISCLOSURES REQUIRED IN THE COVER LETTER; OTHERWISE REMOVE THIS BLOCK].

Thank you for considering our manuscript.

Sincerely,
[CONFIRMED CORRESPONDING AUTHOR]
[CONFIRMED AFFILIATION]
[CONTACT DETAILS REQUIRED BY THE JOURNAL]
```

第三段不要停留在 “will interest your readers”；明确其为何相关。没有定量估计的研究，改写结果句为有依据的主题、方法贡献或论证。

## B. 换刊或正式转投

普通换刊通常按首次投稿向新刊写信，不自动提拒稿史；涉及同一稿件的既往决定、重复提交限制或新刊明确要求时如实披露。出版社正式转投仅在已核实机制与记录时使用下面的起句；不称新刊已邀请返修，除非确有邀请。

```text
[DATE]

Dear Editors,

[FOR A VERIFIED FORMAL TRANSFER ONLY: We submit “[EXACT TITLE]” through the transfer process from [SOURCE JOURNAL].]
[FOR AN ORDINARY NEW SUBMISSION: We submit “[EXACT TITLE]” for consideration as a [ARTICLE TYPE] in [TARGET JOURNAL].]
[STATE THE CENTRAL QUESTION IN TERMS RELEVANT TO THE NEW TARGET].

Using [VERIFIED DESIGN AND DATA], we [ACTUAL ANALYSIS OR INVESTIGATION]. [KEY RESULT, UNCHANGED IN MEANING FROM THE CURRENT MANUSCRIPT]. [SPECIFIC CONTRIBUTION MOST RELEVANT TO THE NEW JOURNAL].

This work addresses [A VERIFIED TOPIC IN THE TARGET JOURNAL'S SCOPE] and provides [SUPPORTED EVIDENCE] for [SPECIFIC READERS OR DECISION]. [NECESSARY LIMITATION OR CONDITION].

[VERIFIED TRANSFER/RELATED-MANUSCRIPT INFORMATION AND OTHER DISCLOSURES, ONLY WHERE REQUIRED].

Thank you for considering our manuscript.

Sincerely,
[CONFIRMED CORRESPONDING AUTHOR AND REQUIRED CONTACT DETAILS]
```

只保留一个起句。重写的是贡献选择和读者问题，不是原始结果；不把旧刊透明评审选项、特刊名称或审稿承诺迁移过来。

## C. 受邀修回提交信

```text
[DATE]

Dear Editors,

Thank you for the opportunity to revise “[EXACT TITLE]” ([VERIFIED MANUSCRIPT ID]). We submit the revised manuscript in response to the decision dated [VERIFIED DATE].

The main revisions are [ACTUAL CHANGE 1], [ACTUAL CHANGE 2], and [ACTUAL CHANGE 3, IF NEEDED]. [BRIEFLY EXPLAIN ANY MATERIAL CHANGE TO THE RESULTS OR INTERPRETATION]. [IF NECESSARY, IDENTIFY AN UNRESOLVED REQUEST AND THE JUSTIFIED ALTERNATIVE ACTUALLY TAKEN].

[IF PREPARED: The accompanying response addresses the editor's and reviewers' comments individually and identifies the corresponding changes.] [IF RELEVANT: EXPLAIN HOW THE REVISED PAPER NOW ADDRESSES THE JOURNAL'S CENTRAL QUESTION, WITHOUT REPEATING THE FULL INITIAL PITCH].

[UPDATED DISCLOSURES OR OTHER INFORMATION REQUIRED FOR THIS ROUND].

We appreciate the time and care devoted to the review of our work.

Sincerely,
[CONFIRMED CORRESPONDING AUTHOR AND REQUIRED CONTACT DETAILS]
```

“已附回复”“已更新结果”和“全部作者同意”分别核实。对只做语言或声明修改的稿件，不说新增分析；对仍有保留意见的情况，不声称所有问题都已完全解决。

## D. 用户要求时才加入的礼貌进度请求

以下可作为单独进度询问信的核心，不自动混进每封修回信：

> We would appreciate an update on the manuscript's progress and any guidance on the next steps. Thank you for your continued consideration.

若用户希望说明耗时，只使用核实的投稿/修回日期与对应事件。具体等待天数核算无误才写；不根据状态页更新时间推断审稿时长，不提出未经用户要求的期限或撤稿威胁。
